SoulTracks policies

Privacy Policy

Version 0.2.1-beta · Effective 2026-09-15

Information SoulTracks processes

SoulTracks processes account information such as email address, username, display name, password credentials handled by the authentication provider, optional biography, and policy acceptances.

Your date of birth is submitted to a trusted registration boundary only to determine whether you are at least 13. SoulTracks then retains the self-attestation time, minimum-age rule, and rule version—not the submitted birth date.

Memories and social activity

The service processes the photos, captions, dates, song references, audiences, tags, Events, likes, comments, Friendships, invitations, reports, and blocks needed to provide the features you use.

Visibility depends on the audience you choose and the product's friendship, Event, blocking, and moderation rules. Public Moments are intended to be viewed by other authenticated Souls.

How information is used

Information is used to operate and secure accounts, deliver requested features, enforce audiences and community rules, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, communicate service messages, and improve the controlled beta.

SoulTracks does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Approved analytics must exclude photos, captions, comments, email, birth information, search text, and authentication tokens.

Service providers and the optional Spotify player

SoulTracks relies on infrastructure and authentication providers such as Supabase and may use an email-delivery provider. These providers process information as needed to supply their services under their own contractual, privacy, and security obligations.

SoulTracks does not contact Spotify merely because you view a Moment. If you deliberately choose to load the official Spotify player, your device connects directly to Spotify's open.spotify.com service. The request necessarily identifies the selected Spotify track and may provide Spotify and its partners with network, device, browser or WebView, locale, and interaction information ordinarily associated with loading and using their service. Spotify states that it and third parties may use cookies or similar technologies. Spotify controls that processing under its own terms and policies.

SoulTracks does not send your SoulTracks authentication token, email address, profile identifier, Moment identifier, photo, caption, tag, audience, or Supabase session to the Spotify player. SoulTracks does not collect player account status, playback position, listening duration, or player interaction analytics. It may record only a coarse content-free load-failure category and app or platform version for reliability, without a track, Moment, or user identifier.

The initial player uses private or nonpersistent WebView storage. SoulTracks may store locally that you accepted the current Spotify-player disclosure, without storing a track or Moment identifier in that preference. You can revoke the choice in Settings. Signing out, switching accounts, or deleting the local account state clears the SoulTracks-held choice. If Spotify authentication or full playback requires persistent browser storage, SoulTracks opens a separately validated Spotify link instead of persisting the player login inside SoulTracks.

Declining to load the player does not limit your ability to view the Moment. You can continue to see the song reference and may choose to open Spotify externally. Spotify controls its own data, account, privacy settings, cookie choices, and deletion processes; deleting SoulTracks data does not itself delete data controlled by Spotify.

Retention, security, and deletion

Information is retained while needed to operate the account, satisfy safety and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain bounded security records. Backups and abuse-prevention records may expire on a documented schedule rather than disappearing immediately.

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Account-deletion behavior will remove active product data according to the published deletion process and retention schedule.

Your choices and contact

You can choose each Moment's audience, leave Events, remove your own contributions, block Souls, and request account deletion when those features are available. Do not use SoulTracks if you are under 13.
