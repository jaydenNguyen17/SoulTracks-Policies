SoulTracks policies

Community Guidelines

Version 0.2.1-beta · Effective 2026-09-14

Build a respectful music-memory community

SoulTracks is for sharing personal memories through photos and music. Treat every Soul with dignity, including when tastes, identities, cultures, and experiences differ from your own.

Do not harass, bully, threaten, stalk, shame, or encourage others to target another person. Hate speech and dehumanizing content based on protected or personal characteristics are prohibited.

Keep people safe

Do not post sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, credible threats, graphic abuse, instructions for serious wrongdoing, or content that promotes self-harm. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on SoulTracks.

Do not reveal private contact, location, financial, medical, or identity information without permission. Never use tags or Events to track, pressure, or expose another person.

Share only what you may share

Post photos and text you created or have permission to use. Respect copyright, publicity, and privacy rights. Song references do not give permission to upload or reproduce recordings, artwork, or lyrics.

Do not impersonate people or organizations, manipulate engagement, distribute scams or malware, or repeatedly post unwanted promotions.

Use song references and provider players only as separate, user-initiated context for a Moment. Do not capture or redistribute provider audio, use a provider player to create a soundtrack or other synchronized visual work, evade provider restrictions, cover or imitate provider controls, or suggest that Spotify, an artist, or another rights holder endorses your Moment. A song reference never excuses content that violates these Guidelines.

Use audiences and Events responsibly

Choose a Moment audience deliberately. Public sharing can reach people you do not know. Event membership never gives permission to copy another Soul's private contribution outside its authorized audience.

Event participants keep ownership and responsibility for their own contributions. Do not pressure participants to add or retain Moments.

Reports and enforcement

SoulTracks may hide or remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, preserve necessary evidence, or contact appropriate authorities when needed to protect people or comply with law. Context, severity, history, and credible risk inform enforcement.

For the controlled beta, report urgent concerns to jayden17nguyen@gmail.com. Do not file knowingly false or retaliatory reports.
